# AI Realist Brand Voice Profile

## Brand Overview

**Mission**: Provide independent, honest, hype-free guidance about AI technology to help decision-makers navigate the noise and make informed choices.

**Vision**: Transform AI industry communication from hype-driven marketing to evidence-based, realistic assessment of technology capabilities and limitations.

**Primary Audience**: Non-technical business leaders and decision-makers who need to integrate AI but struggle to distinguish truth from marketing hype.

**Unique Value**: Independent voice with no agenda to sell specific services, grounded in years of research and industry experience with deep understanding of LLM technology.

## Voice Personality Dimensions

**Moderately Serious (4/10)**: Professional and credible without being dry or academic. Maintains gravitas appropriate for business decision-makers while remaining engaging.

**Balanced Formality (6/10)**: Polished and professional language that's accessible to non-technical audiences. Avoids both overly casual tone and academic jargon.

**Analytical Focus (7/10)**: Strong emphasis on data, evidence, and logical reasoning. Appeals to rational decision-making while acknowledging legitimate concerns and emotions around AI adoption.

**Moderately Skeptical (3/10)**: Healthy skepticism toward AI hype while maintaining sincere, earnest approach to education. Uses wit strategically to highlight absurd claims without being cynical.

**Balanced Authority (5/10)**: Leverages research background and expertise while remaining approachable. Expert guidance delivered as accessible peer rather than distant academic.

## Tone Guidelines

### DO Sound Like:
- **Evidence-based**: "Research shows..." "Studies indicate..." "Data suggests..."
- **Realistic**: "Current capabilities include..." "Limitations to consider..." "Practical applications..."
- **Independent**: "Unbiased assessment..." "No vendor agenda..." "Honest evaluation..."
- **Experienced**: "In practice..." "Real-world implementation..." "Industry experience shows..."
- **Grounded**: "Peer-reviewed findings..." "Reproducible results..." "Transparent methodology..."

### DON'T Sound Like:
- **Hype-driven**: Avoid "groundbreaking," "revolutionary," "game-changing," "RIP Google," "disruption"
- **Vendor-influenced**: No promotional language or biased recommendations
- **Academic elitist**: Avoid inaccessible jargon or condescending tone
- **Overly casual**: Maintain professional credibility appropriate for business leaders
- **Alarmist**: Balanced assessment of risks without fear-mongering

## Vocabulary and Terminology

### Preferred Terms:
- "Evidence-based"
- "Peer-reviewed"
- "Realistic expectations"
- "Practical applications"
- "Current capabilities"
- "Limitations"
- "Transparent methodology"
- "Independent assessment"
- "Real-world implementation"

### Avoid These Terms:
- "Groundbreaking"
- "Revolutionary"
- "Game-changing"
- "RIP [Company]"
- "Disruption"
- "Breakthrough"
- "Cutting-edge" (unless specifically accurate)
- "Next-generation" (marketing speak)

## Example Phrases

### On-Brand Examples:
- "Based on current research, this AI tool shows promise for specific use cases, with important limitations to consider."
- "Our analysis of peer-reviewed studies suggests realistic expectations for implementation."
- "Independent evaluation reveals both capabilities and constraints of this technology."

### Off-Brand Examples:
- "This groundbreaking AI will revolutionize your business!"
- "RIP traditional methods - this changes everything!"
- "Cutting-edge breakthrough disrupts the entire industry!"

## Reference Brands/Voices
- **Gary Marcus**: Thoughtful AI skepticism grounded in cognitive science
- **Emily Bender**: Academic rigor applied to practical AI concerns
- **Andriy Burkov**: Clear, educational approach to complex AI concepts

## Red Flags to Avoid
- Any language that sounds like vendor marketing
- Overpromising AI capabilities
- Dismissing legitimate concerns about AI limitations
- Using buzzwords without substantive backing
- Making claims without citing evidence or research

---

**Brand Voice Summary**: AI Realist speaks as the trusted, independent advisor who cuts through AI hype with evidence-based analysis. We're serious about the technology's potential while honest about its limitations, helping business leaders make informed decisions without vendor bias or academic pretension.
