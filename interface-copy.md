# AI Fake Detection Game - Interface Copy

## Landing Page Copy

### Hero Section
**Headline:** Can You Spot the AI Fakes in Your Workplace?

**Subheadline:** Test your ability to identify AI-generated documents, calls, and media that could fool your business. Learn the red flags that matter.

**CTA Button:** Start Detection Challenge

### Problem Statement
Business professionals face a new reality: AI can now create convincing fake invoices, clone voices for fraudulent calls, and generate realistic documents. The question isn't whether this technology exists—it's whether you can recognize it when it targets your organization.

### How It Works
1. **Face Real Scenarios** - Review documents, audio, and video samples based on actual workplace situations
2. **Make Your Call** - Decide what's real and what's AI-generated
3. **Learn the Red Flags** - Discover specific indicators that reveal AI fakes
4. **Protect Your Business** - Apply detection skills to prevent fraud and deception
